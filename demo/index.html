How this is computed

Standard tool: z = difference of log click rates divided by its noise, √(1/clicks − 1/views) for each version; significant if |z| > 1.96 (p < 0.05).

Real noise: in 1,905 Upworthy tests, versions that were identical before the click differed 1.88 times more (in variance) than the textbook formula allows (95% CI 1.73-2.10). The noise is multiplied by that factor.

B truly better, real gain: empirical Bayes. The distribution of true differences between two versions was estimated from 157,570 version pairs (nonparametric maximum likelihood). Your measurement is combined with it to give the probability that B is better and the likely size of its gain.

Views needed: (1.96 + 0.84)² × 1.88 × (1/p_A + 1/p_B) / log(1 + gain)², at A's click rate.
