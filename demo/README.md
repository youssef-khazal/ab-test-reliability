# Is my winner real?

Enter the views and clicks of two versions of an A/B test. The page shows what a standard tool would
say, how likely the new version is to be truly better once typical gains are taken into account, and how
big its real gain probably is. Calibrated on 17,744 real headline A/B tests from the
[Upworthy Research Archive](https://upworthy.natematias.com/) (CC BY 4.0).

Method and full results: [github.com/youssef-khazal/ab-test-reliability](https://github.com/youssef-khazal/ab-test-reliability).
